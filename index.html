Resident profile · Music & the arts

Astrid Yun

Professional violinist · Norwegian–Chinese

A thoughtful, introverted musician with a deep attachment to classical music and the performing arts. Astrid is often more comfortable expressing herself through music than through conversation.

CLASSICAL MUSICTHEATRE & BALLETCLASSIC LITERATURENORWAY ↔ CHINA
Background

Between two cultures

Astrid is half Chinese and half Norwegian. She was born and raised in Norway, speaks Chinese and travels to China frequently. Both sides of her heritage are part of her life and identity.

Career

A life in music

A professional violinist, Astrid has built her life around music. She loves classical repertoire, theatre and ballet, and has a particular affection for classic literature.

Personality

Quiet, observant, melancholic

Introverted and often melancholic, Astrid tends to observe before she joins in. She values privacy, familiar routines and relationships that develop slowly. She is an atheist.

Home

A wooden house

She lives in a two-storey wooden house. It is also home to a small menagerie: cold-climate dogs such as huskies, rabbits and fish.

Family

A complicated history

Astrid was raised by her Chinese father after her Norwegian mother died shortly after her birth. Her father's second marriage became an unhappy and harmful period in her childhood; he eventually discovered what was happening, ended the marriage and later built a kinder family life with his new wife and their son.

Astrid moved out when her younger half-brother was eighteen. Her family history matters to her, but it does not define the whole of her life.

Story potential

Getting to know her

Build trust through quiet conversations, music, visits to performances and learning about the places that matter to her. Her personal storyline will explore independence, family, artistic ambition and friendship.
